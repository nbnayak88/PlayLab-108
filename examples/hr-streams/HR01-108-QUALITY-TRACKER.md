## Verified scope

- **Implemented prototypes:** PL108-001 (Core HR Decision Sprint), PL108-107 (Workforce Investment Break-Even Simulator). Neither has passed comprehensive browser or subject-matter validation.
- **Contextual prototypes:** PL108-002–009. These use a dedicated HR decision scenario bank and are no longer generic cross-domain questions. Some still simplify the named game mechanic (e.g., Bingo uses a three-correct streak rather than a complete bingo board). Do not label production-ready.
- **Generic placeholders:** Remaining 98 IDs. They appear in the library but do **not** yet satisfy the HR relevance requirement. Their completion must not be treated as validated HR skill.

## Mandatory quality gates per game

1. A realistic fictional HR01 business problem, named learner role, decision constraints and clear outcome.
2. Game-specific mechanics matching the template name, not just multiple-choice substitutions.
3. Consequence model: employee data integrity, security, auditability, cost, cycle time or compliance.
4. Scoring derived from the business objective, with consistent HUD and final result.
5. Meaningful feedback explaining consequences and trade-offs.
6. Timebox, restart, deterministic tests for success and failure, keyboard and mobile QA.
7. Content separated from mechanics; same engine should accept a different subject pack.
8. Peer review and browser validation before changing status to business-validated.
